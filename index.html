Features

Everything Parents Need,
In One App.

Built from the ground up to solve Pakistan's school transport crisis. Every feature designed for safety, transparency, and peace of mind.

Verified Drivers

Background checks, license verification, medical fitness — every driver is vetted before joining Rawaan. No exceptions.

Live GPS Tracking

Real-time vehicle tracking. Know exactly where your child is, get route deviation alerts, and see live ETAs.

Instant Notifications

Pickup alerts, arrival notifications, delay updates, and route changes — parents stay informed in real-time.

Monthly Digital Payments

Pay monthly via the app. No cash, no hassle. Transparent billing with instant receipts and full trip history.

Ratings & Reviews

Community-driven feedback system. Rate every trip and driver to keep quality high and accountability stronger.

SOS Emergency Button

One-tap emergency alert. Instantly notifies parents, Rawaan support, and local authorities with live location.
